Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 09, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Water Can Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information or user data on external servers. Water Can Tracker operates entirely offline.

All data entered into the application is stored locally on your device using a secure SQLite database. This data remains exclusively in your control and is never shared with us or any third parties.

2. App Permissions

The app requires minimal permissions to function. We do not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

3. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data relies on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device updated and secure to protect your local data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
