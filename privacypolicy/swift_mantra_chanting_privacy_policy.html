Privacy Policy

MantraChanting  ·  Effective date: April 13, 2026

MantraChanting ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed and maintained by Mohan Raaj S. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it on your Apple device.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The app does not require account registration, does not use advertising networks, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Mantra titles and metadata (synced from the content catalog)
  • Downloaded audio and video files for offline playback
  • Loop count preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you use iCloud sync (see below).

3. iCloud Sync (Playlists)

If you are signed in to iCloud, your playlists and playlist items are synced across your devices using Apple CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy . We have no access to this data.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by Apple and governed by their privacy policy.

5. Content Delivery (Third-Party Services)

The app fetches the mantra catalog and streams or downloads content from the following external services. Your IP address may be visible to these services per their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sheets / Google Drive — mantra catalog and downloadable audio/video files (Google Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube — streaming playback and thumbnail images (Google Privacy Policy)

We do not pass any personal information to these services. Requests are read-only and solely for delivering app content.

6. Push Notifications

The app is configured to support push notifications via Apple Push Notification service (APNs). If enabled in a future update, you will be asked for permission before any notifications are sent. No personal data is associated with notification tokens.

7. Children's Privacy

MantraChanting is rated 4+ and contains no age-restricted content. We do not knowingly collect any information from users of any age.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All data stored on-device can be removed by deleting the app. iCloud playlist data can be deleted from your iCloud account settings. We hold no data on our own servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
