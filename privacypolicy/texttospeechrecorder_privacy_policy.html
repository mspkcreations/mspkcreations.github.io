Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Text to Speech Recorder ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by MSPK Creations, handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information, text inputs, or voice recordings on external servers. Text to Speech Recorder processes your data entirely on your device.

All text entered and audio generated within the application is handled and saved locally. This data remains exclusively in your control and is never shared with us or any third parties.

2. App Permissions

The app requires minimal permissions necessary for its core functionality, such as storage access to save your generated audio files locally. We only request permissions essential for the app to operate.

3. Data Security

Since all data and recordings are stored locally on your device, the security of your data relies on your device's built-in security features. We recommend keeping your device protected with standard security measures like a screen lock.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
