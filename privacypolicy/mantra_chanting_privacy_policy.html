Privacy Policy

Mantra Chanting  ·  Last updated: March 19, 2026

🛡️

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. No account is required. Your data stays on your device, always.

🙏 About This App

Mantra Chanting is a devotional audio app for streaming and offline chanting practice. It is developed and maintained by mspk Creations.

This Privacy Policy describes what information the app accesses and why. By using the app you agree to this policy.

📦 Data Stored on Your Device

All data the app creates stays locally on your device and is never transmitted to us.

  • Playlists & preferences Playlists you create, loop counts, and app settings stored as a local JSON file.
  • Downloaded media Audio and video files you choose to download for offline chanting.
  • Thumbnail cache Locally cached cover images for chant titles.

We have no access to this data. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

🌐 How We Use the Internet

The app connects to the internet only for these specific purposes:

  • Content catalog sync Fetches the public list of mantras and chants from a read-only Google Spreadsheet. No personal data is sent.
  • Media download Downloads audio/video files that you explicitly request for offline use.
  • YouTube streaming Streams chant videos via an embedded YouTube player. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • In-app purchase Connects to Google Play Billing to process the optional one-time premium upgrade. No payment data is handled by us.

🔗 Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

  • YouTube / Google LLC Used to stream devotional videos. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing Used to offer and process the optional one-time premium purchase. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

🔐 App Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions, each with a specific purpose:

  • INTERNET Sync the content catalog, stream YouTube video, and download media files.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE Run the audio player as a foreground service so playback continues when you switch apps.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_
    MEDIA_PLAYBACK     Classify the foreground service as media playback for OS-level optimization.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show playback controls and loop progress in the notification shade.
  • WAKE_LOCK Keep audio playing without interruption when the screen turns off.

🌸 Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app poses no privacy risk to minors.

📋 Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
