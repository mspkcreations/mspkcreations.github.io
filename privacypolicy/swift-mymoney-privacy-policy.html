My Money - Track Expenses

Privacy Policy

Effective April 6, 2026

My Money - Track Expenses (“the App”) is developed by Mohan Raaj S (“Developer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information when you use it.

Summary: My Money does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or third parties. Your data is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced privately via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to your other devices.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal information. My Money stores all data — wallets, transactions, profiles, and settings — locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate any servers and have no access to your data.

No Accounts Required

My Money does not require you to create an account or sign in with us. There is no developer-operated server, no user profile, and no authentication of any kind beyond your existing Apple ID for iCloud sync.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use any analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising frameworks. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, My Money uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. This means the following data is stored in your private iCloud account:

  • Wallet names, types, icons, and sort order
  • Transaction records (expenses, income, transfers) including amounts, categories, dates, and notes
  • Transaction entries (ledger lines linking transactions to wallets)
  • Profile names, currency settings, and form defaults

This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is accessible only to you. We have no ability to read, access, or extract your iCloud data. iCloud sync is managed entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable iCloud sync for this app in your device's Settings under iCloud.

In-App Purchases

My Money offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited transactions. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, billing information, or personal data as part of this transaction.

Third-Party Services

My Money uses no third-party SDKs, services, or APIs. The only external integrations are Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases and Apple's CloudKit for iCloud sync, both of which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

The following information is stored locally on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced to your private iCloud account:

  • Wallet names, types, and balances
  • Transaction records (expenses, income, transfers)
  • Profile names and currency settings
  • App preferences and form defaults

Premium purchase status is managed by Apple through the App Store and is not stored in iCloud by the app.

Children's Privacy

My Money does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of My Money after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
