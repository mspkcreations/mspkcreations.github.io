Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Video Convertor ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by MSPK Creations, handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Local Video Processing

We do not collect, transmit, or store your videos, personal information, or user data on external servers. Video Convertor processes all video files entirely on your local device.

Your media remains exclusively in your control and is never shared with us or any third parties.

2. App Permissions

The app requires device storage or media permissions solely to access the video files you choose to convert and to save the final output back to your device. We do not access files outside of this core functionality.

3. Data Security

Because all data and video processing occur locally, the security of your files relies on your device's built-in security features. We recommend keeping your device protected with standard security measures like a screen lock.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
