Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sleep Time Tracker ("we", "us", or "our"), developed by MSPK Creations, handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information or user data on external servers. Sleep Time Tracker operates entirely on your device.

All sleep tracking data entered into the application is stored locally. This data remains exclusively in your control and is never shared with us or any third parties.

2. App Permissions

The app requires minimal permissions necessary for its core functionality. We do not unnecessarily request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

3. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data relies on the security of your device itself. We recommend utilizing your device's built-in security features (like screen locks) to protect your local data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.
