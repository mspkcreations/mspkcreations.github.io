Stopwatch: Session Tracker

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Overview

Stopwatch: Session Tracker ("the App") is a multi-athlete lap, split, and workout session timing application designed for coaches, athletes, and everyday training. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App uses, how it is stored, and your rights.

Stopwatch: Session Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data with any third party. All your data stays on your device and, optionally, your personal iCloud account.

Data You Enter

The App allows you to create and manage:

  • Athlete roster profiles (names, initials, color badges, and optional avatars)
  • Timed heats, lap splits, and finish order records
  • Workout sessions, session notes, and custom timing preferences

All data you enter is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device except through iCloud sync (see below) or result exports that you initiate yourself.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud and have iCloud Drive enabled, the App may sync your data across your personal Apple devices using CloudKit — Apple's private cloud database service. Your data is stored in your own iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud data.

You can disable iCloud sync at any time in your device's Settings app under iCloud › Session Timer.

Export & Sharing

The App lets you export and share race result summary cards and split data. Exported files and images are shared only to destinations you explicitly choose (Messages, Mail, Files app, AirDrop, etc.). We have no access to exported content.

Analytics & Tracking

Stopwatch: Session Tracker does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking technologies. We do not collect crash reports, usage metrics, or any telemetry data from your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any of your data with third parties. Your athlete profiles, lap times, and workout history are yours alone.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained only for as long as the App is installed on your device. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data. To remove iCloud-synced data, delete the App on all your devices or manage your iCloud storage in Apple's Settings.

Children's Privacy

Stopwatch: Session Tracker does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App or on our support page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
