Privacy Policy

Bedtime Stories & Kids Books  ·  Last updated: April 2026

The short version: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you or your child. Ever.

Who We Are

Bedtime Stories & Kids Books is developed by Mohan Raaj S, an independent developer. Contact: mohan.raaj.s@gmail.com

What We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Any information about your child

What Stays on Your Device

All story content, playlists, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Downloaded stories are saved to your device's local storage and never uploaded to any server controlled by us.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your playlists sync across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Story Catalog

The app fetches a list of available stories from a publicly accessible Google Sheet (titles, authors, age groups). No personal data is sent during this request — it is a read-only, anonymous fetch.

Story Downloads

Downloading a story fetches a file from Google Drive. No personal data is attached to this request. We do not log or track which stories are downloaded.

YouTube Thumbnails

Some stories display a thumbnail image fetched from YouTube. This is a standard image request and does not involve any personal data. It is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchase

Premium unlocks are processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not see or store any payment or billing information. Purchase history is managed by your Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children. We take that seriously. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults. The app contains no ads, no social features, no sign-in, and no analytics SDK.

Third-Party Services

The app interacts with the following external services solely to deliver content:

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — playlist sync across your own devices
  • Google Sheets — read-only story catalog
  • Google Drive — story file downloads
  • YouTube — thumbnail images only

None of these interactions include personal data originating from you or your child.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Reach us at mohan.raaj.s@gmail.com or visit our support page.
